A complete parking intelligence platform
🤖
Auto-Detect Slot Boundaries
Upload any parking lot image or video — YOLO11x detects vehicles and automatically infers slot polygons including empty spaces. No manual clicking required for standard lots.
🎬
Real-Time Video Processing
Process parking lot videos frame-by-frame with live streaming results. See occupancy change over time with an interactive chart and frame gallery.
📐
Spatial IoU Mapping
Each parking slot is defined as a polygon. Occupancy is determined by Intersection over Union between detected vehicle bounding boxes and slot polygons — accurate for any camera angle.
📊
Historical Analytics
Track peak hours, weather impact, and daily occupancy trends from the PKLot dataset. Filter by date range and export CSV reports.
✏️
Manual Slot Editor
For unusual layouts, draw slot polygons directly on the image in your browser. Click 4 corners per slot — slots are saved at original resolution and reused automatically.
🎯
Model Evaluation
Evaluate YOLO11x accuracy against PKLot XML ground truth. View Precision, Recall, F1, confusion matrix, and per-slot accuracy breakdown.
